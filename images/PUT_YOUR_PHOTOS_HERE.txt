Put your photos in this folder.

Name them to match what script.js expects (see the CONFIG.photos array
near the top of script.js):

    photo1.jpeg
    photo2.jpeg
    photo3.jpeg
    photo4.jpeg

You can use .jpg, .png, or .webp — just update the file extension in
script.js to match (e.g. "images/photo1.png").

You can also add more than 4 photos, or fewer. Just add/remove entries
in the CONFIG.photos array in script.js. Each entry looks like:

    { src: "images/photo5.jpg", title: "", caption: "" }

title and caption are optional — leave them as "" if you don't want
a caption under that photo.

Until real photos are added, the gallery on the final page will show
a tasteful placeholder card telling you which file is missing — it
will not look broken, so you can test the whole site before your
photos are ready.

Delete this text file whenever you want — it's not read by the site.
